The Offering

A Strategic Asset at the
Heart of the Galleria

Hodges Ward Elliott is pleased to present the opportunity to acquire the fee-simple interest in the 317-room Sheraton Dallas by the Galleria, strategically located at one of the busiest intersections in the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex, across the street from the iconic Galleria Dallas, and surrounded by over 40 million square feet of office and retail space in a three-mile radius.

The Galleria, a landmark retail, dining, entertainment, and office development, has anchored North Dallas for more than four decades, receiving over 12 million visitors per year and approximately 7,000 office professionals per day.

The surrounding area is continuing to scale with an influx of major office leases, retail tenants, and new development, creating a compelling long-term demand outlook for the Hotel. Most recently, the NBA's Dallas Mavericks have selected a nearby 104-acre site to build a new arena, headquarters, and mixed-use entertainment district — a transformational investment less than a mile from the Property.

Investment Highlights

Why Dallas. Why Now.

A convergence of location, brand, and market timing defines this offering.

At the Doorstep of the Galleria

Situated directly across the Tollway from the iconic Galleria Dallas — a 1.4-million-square-foot super-regional mall receiving over 12 million annual visitors, alongside three office towers hosting 7,000 professionals daily.

Transformational Mavericks Development

The Dallas Mavericks have selected a 104-acre site less than a mile away to build a new arena, headquarters, and mixed-use entertainment district — a major economic catalyst targeting the 2031-32 NBA season.

Deep Corporate Demand Base

Approximately 29 million square feet of office space within a three-mile radius, with major tenants including Amazon, Ryan, Marsh, RSM, AECOM, and Burns & McDonnell across diversified industries.

Exceptional DFW Connectivity

Located at the intersection of the Dallas North Tollway and I-635, providing access to Downtown Dallas, DFW Airport (15 min), and North Texas employment centers. Approximately 400,000 vehicles pass daily.

4th-Largest U.S. Metro Area

The DFW metroplex (8.5M population) leads the nation in employment growth with 450,000 net new jobs this decade, 24 Fortune 500 headquarters, and was ranked the #1 U.S. investment market for five consecutive years.

Renovation Upside

Institutionally maintained with a recently renovated Water Fire Grille, the Property offers meaningful RevPAR upside through targeted guest room renovations to drive enhanced rate performance and competitive positioning.

Clean Investment Offering

Fee-simple interest offered free and clear of existing management and financing, providing maximum optionality for new ownership in future operations and capitalization.
